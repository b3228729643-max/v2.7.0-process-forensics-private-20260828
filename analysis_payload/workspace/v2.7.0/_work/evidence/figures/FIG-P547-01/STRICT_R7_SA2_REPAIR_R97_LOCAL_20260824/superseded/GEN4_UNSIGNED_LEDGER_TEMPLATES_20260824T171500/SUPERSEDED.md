# Superseded unsigned ledger templates

These seven CSV files are immutable copies of the machine-generated GEN4 manual-review templates before human inspection. Their `PENDING_MANUAL` fields are template placeholders and are not active acceptance evidence.

After every referenced contact sheet and required native 1x/8x card was opened manually the active files at their original `final_audit` paths were replaced by explicit row-by-row signed ledgers. This directory is retained only to make that transition auditable and must not be counted as unresolved review state.
