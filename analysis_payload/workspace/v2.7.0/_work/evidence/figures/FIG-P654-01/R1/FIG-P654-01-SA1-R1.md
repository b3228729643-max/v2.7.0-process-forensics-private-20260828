## findings

### 数学与数值复算

设先验为

\[
p(\boldsymbol\theta\mid\boldsymbol\alpha)
\propto \prod_i\theta_i^{\alpha_i-1},
\]

多项似然核为

\[
p(\boldsymbol n\mid\boldsymbol\theta)
\propto \prod_i\theta_i^{n_i}.
\]

二者相乘得到

\[
p(\boldsymbol\theta\mid\boldsymbol n,\boldsymbol\alpha)
\propto
\prod_i\theta_i^{\alpha_i+n_i-1},
\]

所以

\[
\boldsymbol\Theta\mid\boldsymbol n,\boldsymbol\alpha
\sim\operatorname{Dir}(\boldsymbol\alpha+\boldsymbol n).
\]

令 $\alpha_0=\sum_i\alpha_i$、$N=\sum_i n_i$，则

\[
\Pr(Y_{N+1}=i\mid\boldsymbol n,\boldsymbol\alpha)
=\mathbb E[\Theta_i\mid\boldsymbol n,\boldsymbol\alpha]
=\frac{\alpha_i+n_i}{\alpha_0+N}.
\]

并且

\[
\sum_i\frac{\alpha_i+n_i}{\alpha_0+N}=1.
\]

这与 `V5-C05.tex:539-560`、`:607-635` 完全一致。用正文示例 $\boldsymbol\alpha=(2,3,5)$、$\boldsymbol n=(4,1,0)$ 复核，可得后验 $\operatorname{Dir}(6,4,5)$ 和预测 $(2/5,4/15,1/3)$，分量和为 1。
