## Mathematical and scope audit

- The C07 column convention is consistent throughout:
  \(A_{ij}>0\) denotes \(j\to i\),
  \(c_j=\sum_iA_{ij}\) is the source \(j\)'s weighted outdegree, and for
  \(c_j>0\), \(M_{ij}=A_{ij}/c_j\). Thus
  \(r_i^{(t+1)}=\sum_jM_{ij}r_j^{(t)}
  =\sum_{j:j\to i}M_{ij}r_j^{(t)}\) correctly aggregates source-column
  contributions into target row \(i\).
- In the non-weighted, no-dangling scope, the source correctly states
  \(M_{ij}=1/\deg^+(j)\) iff \(j\to i\), otherwise zero. The denominator
  belongs to the source, not the target.
- The three source-to-target draw commands share the same inbound style and
  line width. Each carries only the symbolic
  \(M_{ij_q}r_{j_q}^{(t)}\) contribution, and the formula card explicitly
  says that no values are supplied and the equal widths do not encode
  contribution magnitude. No numeric amount is fabricated.
- The matrix scopes are correctly fenced. The upper part is explicitly
  “basic PageRank, no dangling, \(S=M\)”. In the general construction,
  \(M\) is the unrepaired along-link matrix,
  \(S=M+\boldsymbol v\boldsymbol a^{\mathsf T}\) replaces dangling columns,
  and
  \(G=dS+(1-d)\boldsymbol v\boldsymbol1^{\mathsf T}\) adds teleportation.
  If \(c_j=0\), \(S_{ij}=v_i>0\) can occur without \(j\to i\); therefore
  positive entries of repaired \(S/G\) cannot be interpreted as original
  inbound links. The source, caption, boundary card, body and read-check
  agree on this limitation.
