# Evidence index

- `IDENTITY.md`: fixed instance identity, boundary, and independent page mapping.
- `SA3_REPORT.md`: self-contained SA3 findings and local-only conclusion.
- `RESULT.json`: machine-readable result and exact denominators.
- `HANDOFF.md`: concise sealed handoff.
- `renders/`: complete page, figure, standalone, grayscale, and measurement views derived from the official PDF.
- `machine/`: identity, object, glyph, drawing-path, clip, peer, safe-name, pair, render, absence, sheet, and cross-check inventories.
- `masks/`: individual glyph and graphic masks.
- `cards/glyph/`: 131 individual nearest-neighbor 8x glyph triptychs.
- `cards/glyph_contact_sheets/`: 33 exact contact sheets covering every glyph card.
- `cards/graphic/`: 23 individual graphic triptychs including two explicit background exclusions.
- `cards/critical_pair_contact_sheets/`: 11 exact sheets covering every critical pair.
- `pairs/`: six evidence images for each of 42 critical pairs.
- `manual/`: individually authored glyph, pair, clip, graphic, render, parent, peer/role, semantic, and hard-gate ledgers.
- `MANIFEST.json`: resolved-path file inventory and recordset seal; intentionally excludes itself and `WRITE_STOPPED`.
- `WRITE_STOPPED`: strictly latest final marker and seal hashes.
