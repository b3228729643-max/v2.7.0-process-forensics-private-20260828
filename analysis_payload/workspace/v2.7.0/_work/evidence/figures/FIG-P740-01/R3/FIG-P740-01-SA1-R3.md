## Independent mathematical and semantic check

### Dimensions and directed conditional Gaussian

The chapter defines
\[
X\in\mathbb R^{M\times N},\qquad
W\in\mathbb R^{M\times K},\qquad
H\in\mathbb R^{K\times N}.
\]
Since the source and chapter identify \(h_n\) as the \(n\)-th column of \(H\),
\[
h_n\in\mathbb R^K,\qquad Wh_n\in\mathbb R^M,\qquad
x_n=X_{:n}\in\mathbb R^M.
\]
Thus the displayed conditional
\[
x_n\mid W,h_n\sim\mathcal N(Wh_n,\sigma^2 I)
\]
has the correct mean direction and dimension (with \(I\) the \(M\times M\) identity in this context). In the TikZ source, the probability-edge style has an arrow tip and the two drawings are (w)--(x) and (h)--(x), so both \(W\to x_n\) and \(h_n\to x_n\) have the required direction; they are not undirected algebraic equalities.
