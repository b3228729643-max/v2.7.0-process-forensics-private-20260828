## findings

### F-01 — discrete-CDF mathematics and data are correct (PASS)

The source embeds the support points \(1,2,3,4\), PMF masses \((0.15,0.30,0.35,0.20)\), and CDF levels \(0,0.15,0.45,0.80,1\). The adjacent CDF differences are respectively \(0.15,0.30,0.35,0.20\), all non-negative and summing to 1.

The rendered vector path starts at the zero level, jumps vertically at each support point, and then continues horizontally to the right. Filled dots are placed at \((1,.15),(2,.45),(3,.80),(4,1)\); open dots are placed at \((1,0),(2,.15),(3,.45),(4,.80)\). Thus the graph is right-continuous, monotone non-decreasing, starts at 0 in the displayed left tail, and reaches 1 in the displayed right tail. It is not a density plot: the upper panel is labelled \(F_X(t)\), the lower panel is labelled \(p_X(t)\), and the lower y-comb plot explicitly supplies the point masses.

### F-02 — body, data, ticks, support and caption are consistent (PASS)

The chapter defines \(F_X(t)=P(X\le t)\), states monotonicity, right-continuity, and the two endpoint limits, then inputs the figure. Its immediately following reading instruction says that jump sizes recover probability masses, masses accumulate to the CDF, the right-continuous convention selects the upper endpoint, and monotonicity plus terminal value 1 are legality checks.

The plotted support ticks \(1,2,3,4\), upper CDF levels, lower PMF heights, \(p_i\) labels, caption, chapter reference, figure_sources.json entry, and figure_manifest.csv row all agree:

> 离散随机变量的分布函数：跳跃高度等于对应点的概率质量

### F-03 — source font contract is violated (FAIL)

The manifest requires source-level visible text at or above 9.5pt. The canonical figure instead specifies:

- mass labels: 9.2pt;
- note text: 9.2pt;
- upper-axis tick labels: 8.8pt;
- upper-axis labels: 9.4pt;
- lower-axis tick labels: 8.6pt;
- lower-axis labels: 9.4pt.

Candidate PDF text inspection correspondingly shows approximately 9pt key graph text (with naturally smaller mathematical subscripts). The chart width is .82\linewidth and the source contains no scale, transform shape, resizebox, scalebox, or other whole-figure reduction. Density and geometry therefore pass, but the source-font hard requirement fails.

### F-04 — layout and reading order are sound; no split is justified (PASS)

The R02 physical-page evidence shows the proof, complete paired chart, caption, direct two-way reading sentence, and subsequent self-checks/section transition in a coherent order. Neither panel is clipped; the caption remains directly below the chart; no overlap or collision is visible. The two panels fit in the effective text area and should remain together.

### F-05 — grayscale design is structurally redundant, but current grayscale evidence is missing (EVIDENCE-BLOCKED)

The source does not rely solely on colour: the CDF is a solid staircase with explicit open/filled endpoint contrast, the PMF is a y-comb with filled point markers, and vertical guides are dashed. Those structural encodings should remain distinguishable in grayscale.

However, there is no FIG-P067-01-specific R1 evidence directory containing a current crop or grayscale rendering. The central figure manifest's historical “通过（Gate C 200 dpi灰度接触表）” entry is not adequate replacement evidence for this independent closure.

> 对象—关系—结论：离散随机变量的分布函数：跳跃高度等于对应点的概率质量

Output-level alt must therefore be treated as unproved/failed rather than accepted from metadata alone.
